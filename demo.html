About the Huffman comparison

Ordinary Huffman coding over GPT-2 tokens, using one frequency table from the entire input. The count covers the same preceding tokens as our encoding. Payload bits only; codebook, length metadata, and final byte padding excluded.

How the intervals work

The full vocabulary is mapped to positive integer counts. Bounds stay on the original [0, 1) scale and shrink at every step; the diagram zooms into the preceding interval. Exact integer fractions prevent underflow. Decimal endpoints are rounded outward with increasing precision. Expand “Inspect current and new bounds” to see the full numbers. Model and illustrated probabilities may differ slightly because of quantization.
